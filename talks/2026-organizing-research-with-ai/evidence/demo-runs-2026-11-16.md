## Papers on vacancy diffusion in concentrated Ni-Fe-Cr

No topic line in `papers/wiki.md` covers this, so I searched the catalogue. Two held papers are directly on it, both on the same fcc 55Fe-28Ni-17Cr alloy:

- **Alecsandre Sauvé-Lacoursière (2022)**, "Unexpected role of prefactors in defects diffusion: The case of vacancies in the 55Fe-28Ni-17Cr concentrated solid-solution alloys", Acta Materialia 237, 118153. DOI: 10.1016/j.actamat.2022.118153
  - From the note: "ARTn event search in k-ART (off-lattice KMC) … 22 cells of 2047 atoms, one vacancy each … 243 events, barriers < 5 eV", with hTST (Vineyard) prefactors.
  - Note: `papers/pub3/notes/Sauve-Lacoursiere2022.md`
- **Joseph Lefèvre López (2024)**, "Harmonic transition state theory applied to vacancy diffusion pre-exponential factors in a concentrated solid-solution alloy", Physical Review Materials 8(1):013609. DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.8.013609
  - From the note: "fcc 55Fe-28Ni-17Cr (at.%) CSA, one vacancy, 2047-atom periodic box … 500 K only. k-ART off-lattice KMC; Bonny-2013 EAM", comparing constant ν = 10¹³ s⁻¹ against per-event hTST prefactors.
  - Note: `papers/pub3/notes/LefevreLopez2024.md`

One adjacent paper covers vacancies in the same alloy family, but the part of the note I can see is about defect energetics, not diffusion:

- **J. B. Piochaud (2014)**, "First-principles study of point defects in an fcc Fe-10Ni-20Cr model alloy", Physical Review B 89(2):024101. DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.89.024101
  - From the note: "DFT only … 256-atom SQS (180 Fe, 50 Cr, 26 Ni) … vacancy (one Fe removed; 60 sites)", plus ⟨100⟩ dumbbells.
  - Note: `papers/pub1/notes/Piochaud2014.md`

All three notes are marked "stand-in note built from the paper-library catalogue summary; not a verified reading note", and their summaries are cut off mid-sentence. The other vacancy-diffusion hits in the catalogue are binary Ni-Fe, dilute Ni-Cr, or other systems, so I left them out.

## KMC models where the diffusion is surface diffusion only by inference

Neither note uses the words "surface diffusion"; the readers inferred it from the bond-counting rules.

- **Artymowicz 2007** (pub2), parting-limit lattice KMC: `pub2/notes/Artymowicz2007.md`
  - The diffusion rate is 10¹³·exp(−n·E_b/kT) with E_b = 0.15 eV per bond.
  - An atom needs at least 3 dangling bonds to move, though the note is inconsistent on whether that rule covers diffusion or only dissolution.
  - The diffusion prefactor is varied, but the note is cut off before saying what that shows.
- **Sandupatla 2021** (pub5), nanoporosity evolution and coarsening in Ag-Au: `pub5/notes/Sandupatla2021.md`
  - Bond-counting KMC with ν_diffusion = ν_dissolution = 2 × 10⁹ s⁻¹, ε = 0.25 eV and T = 334 K.
  - The diffusion prefactor was lowered from 10¹² s⁻¹ so that dissolution is picked about once per 100 hops.
